# TraceTrack — AI-First Operational Telemetry & Frontline Compliance

**[TraceTrack](https://www.tracetrackapp.com)** is the AI-native operations, compliance, and live telemetry platform built for manufacturing, food safety, warehousing, logistics, clinical, and hospitality facilities.

Legacy inspection software charges $24–$100+ per user seat, forcing plants to share generic tablet logins that ruin audit trails and diagnose operational failures from yesterday's static PDF reports. TraceTrack replaces per-seat digital clipboards with hands-free voice execution, zero-login guest runs, and sub-second facility telemetry.

---

### Core Architecture & Capabilities

* **Look Ma, No Hands (Ambient Voice AI):** Walk the floor and talk to Trace to generate OSHA, FDA, ISO, and HACCP-compliant inspection tracks or execute runs hands-free through heavy PPE in English, Spanish, or French.
* **$0 Per-Seat Fees (Zero-Account Guest Runs):** License by physical site and workspace ($350/mo base with unlimited users and unlimited AI). Frontline crews and outside contractors execute runs via secure browser links or 4-digit PIN kiosks—sealed with hardware GPS stamps and **Biometric Selfie Signatures**.
* **The Pit Wall & $0 Smart-TV Andon Boards:** Watch operational inputs and red-line exceptions stream live as they happen, and cast real-time production line Andon boards to any standard TV at zero hardware cost.
* **Decoupled Triage & 8-Stage Human Factors Forensics:** Floor workers flag anomalies in 2 seconds and return to work. Issues route to Management Triage, where Trace coaches supervisors through Socratic 8-stage root-cause investigations.
* **Auditor Walled Gardens & Conversational Analytics:** Generate time-expiring, cryptographically scoped read-only portals for external auditors, or interrogate floor trends by voice using Rearview Mirror Pareto charts.
